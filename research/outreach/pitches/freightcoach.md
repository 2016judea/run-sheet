I spent five years at the largest parcel network, the last of it pulling apart truckload tours to find where empty miles came from. The finding: split deadhead by cause and compare tours with each cause to tours without it. Some causes cost money. Some come with cheaper tours and are not the target. And the long empty legs were planned by systems that never saw each other, not by drivers.

That is door six of ten ideas I wrote down for small fleets and DCs, free: https://dock-doors.vercel.app/doors/size-it-before-you-build-it/
