SCMR's contributor page asks for thought leadership that is "topic- or trend-driven and not product-, company-, or solution-focused." I have one that sells nothing.

I spent five years building real-time staffing software for the largest parcel network in the country. The most useful thing I learned: sending a body to a full lane only pays when that lane is the only place the freight can go. Where a destination is mirrored across several chutes, the same move measures as nothing. We ran the same rule, the same signal and the same hourly A/B design on two kinds of floor. Decisive on one, a null on the other.

Proposed piece, about 1,100 words:
1. The lead walking the wall all shift is a person polling a signal the building already emits.
2. Dead ends vs mirrors, and why the null result was the better finding.
3. The cheapest win: renaming a station to match the paint on the floor nearly doubled adherence in a day.
4. What any DC can measure Monday with the data it already owns.
